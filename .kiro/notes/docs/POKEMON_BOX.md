# The Pokémon Box

Owner's answers (26 October 2026, to the five questions after his G1R Deluxe screenshots):
1 C (a shared box AND a trade shortcut), 2 C (on the game card AND in the in-game menu),
3 A+B+C (Red/Blue/Yellow, Gold/Silver/Crystal, Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald/FireRed/LeafGreen),
4 A (moving between generations allowed, with a preview of every change), 5 A (the box first,
G1R's other tools later). "I don't want it to be the main thing", a cool extra: it only shows on a
Pokémon game's card and in its pause menu.

## Where it is
- Engine: `crates/emulator-bridge/src/pokebox/` (`save.rs` the PC boxes in each game's save with
  their checksums, `mon.rs` one Pokémon in each generation's form and the conversions, `text.rs` the
  letters, `data.rs` generated by `scripts/pokebox-data.py` from PokeAPI's CSVs, BSD licence).
  Swift-facing: `src/uniffi_pokebox.rs`.
- App: `native/ios/PokemonBox.swift` (the sheet, `PokemonGames`, `EngineHost.openPokemonBox`),
  wired in `LibraryShell.swift` (the sheet), `LibraryExtras.swift` (card button), `NewLookPause.swift`
  + `PlayerScreen.swift` (pause tile and the old look's menu), `SaveStates.swift`
  (`setAsideAutoSaveForPokemonBox`), `ContinuumApp.swift` (three EngineHost vars), `project.yml`.
- The Box's own file: Application Support/PokemonBox/box.bin (50 boxes of 30).

## Rules it keeps
- Same generation: the Pokémon is copied byte for byte. Across: through `Mon`, with each change as
  a plain line in the preview (moves the game lacks, items, Nature/Ability, DVs<->IVs, shiny and
  gender kept, the Unown letter kept, friendship, "traded" obedience). Refused with a reason:
  species the target generation lacks, eggs across generations, no moves left, Bad Eggs across.
- Trades evolve the trade evolutions (Kadabra etc., and Gen 2/3 item ones; Everstone stops it).
- Every change: journal first (`pending.journal`, paths kept relative to Application Support),
  `<save>.before-box` copy, then the files; finished on the next open if cut off.
- The game's auto-save is moved into a free numbered slot ("before the Pokémon Box") before its save
  changes, because resuming it would bring back the old save. From the pause menu the game closes
  first and reopens from its title screen when its save changed.
- Only English and European cartridges (Gen 2: English only); Japanese ones are not offered.

## Tools (26 October afternoon, "don't stop, continue")
Picked by me from G1R Deluxe's list, the ones whose meaning is plain from the name (change freely):
Boxes (Move / Trade), Team (a game's team; a team Pokémon into the Box, never the last one, or a
Box Pokémon into the team with its stats worked out: `mon::stats`, base stats from PokeAPI's
pokemon_stats_past so they are the Gen 1-3 values),
List (every Pokémon in the Box, every game's PCs and teams; search, sort by number/level/name),
Dashboard (totals, kinds of 386/251/151, shinies, eggs, where they are), Arrange (sort a Box box,
or move a Pokémon to a chosen place, swapping). Engine: `save::read_party`/`take_party`,
`Change::PartyToStorage`, `InStorageTo`, `Sort`.

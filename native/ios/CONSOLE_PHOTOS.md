# Console photos

The console pictures on the New look's Home (Settings → LOOK → New, the SYSTEMS row) are
photos from Wikimedia Commons. Each was downloaded at 960 px, its white background removed
where it had one, trimmed and placed on a transparent 384 × 384 square. Nothing else was changed.
They live in `native/ios/Assets.xcassets/console-<system>.imageset`.

Public domain and CC0 photos need no credit; they are listed anyway. CC BY and CC BY-SA photos
are used under those licences: the author and the licence are named here, and the changed
versions in this repository are under the same licence as the original.
